13 / Answers

Questions creators ask before checkout.

Short, practical answers without the marketing fog.

Should I choose dynamic or condenser?

Dynamic microphones are often easier in untreated rooms because they can reject more ambient sound. Condensers can capture more detail when the recording space is controlled.

What should I compare besides frequency response?

Consider polar pattern, self-noise, sensitivity, preamp requirements, connectivity, monitoring, room conditions and how you actually record.

Why does noise cancellation matter?

For headphones, ANC can reduce continuous external noise. For microphones, room rejection and placement matter more directly to recorded speech.
